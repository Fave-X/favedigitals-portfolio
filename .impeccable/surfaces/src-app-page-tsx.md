## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is an investigator's case file. It refuses the category-default portfolio (hero, project cards, stat tiles): the work is presented as exhibits in an open file, and the first thing a visitor sees is the evidence itself.

OWN-WORLD: A dossier read at night. Midnight folder-board ground, case sheets in a slightly warmer charcoal, warm paper-white ink, typed field values for case data, condensed grotesque case titles, file tabs as navigation, exhibit tags and status stamps. One accent, red pen, used only on things you can click and on circled finding marks.

STORY: The visitor reads the file header and the thesis line, watches Exhibit A's money trail (Binance hack, Tron leg) end at 9 addresses the FBI list missed, opens the case, then scans the exhibit log, reads the investigator profile, and contacts her or downloads the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: File header strip of typed fields across the top. Thesis line large, left, in condensed grotesque. Below it, full width, Exhibit A: the trail as a horizontal chain of hops with amounts, ending in a red-circled finding and an Open case link. CV and Copy email actions sit in the header strip's right end and under the thesis.
